SUPPORT OUR MISSION

Your support can help Ibaji Restoration Foundation advance good governance, accountability, responsible leadership and sustainable community development.

Support Ibaji Restoration Foundation

Every contribution can help strengthen meaningful community initiatives and advocacy.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation welcomes support from individuals, organizations, institutions, community groups and development partners who share our commitment to positive community development and responsible citizenship.

Donations may support appropriate Foundation activities, programmes, awareness initiatives and community engagement.

Why Support Us?

Your support can contribute to important areas of the Foundation's work.

📢

Advocacy

Support awareness and advocacy for good governance, accountability and responsible leadership.

🤝

Community Development

Support constructive community engagement and sustainable development initiatives.

👥

Citizen Participation

Help encourage citizens to participate positively in community and civic affairs.
