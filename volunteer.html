BECOME A VOLUNTEER

Join Ibaji Restoration Foundation and contribute your time, skills and ideas towards good governance, accountability, citizen participation and sustainable community development.

Volunteer With Us

Every responsible contribution can make a difference.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation welcomes individuals who are interested in supporting positive change and constructive community engagement.

Volunteers may contribute through community outreach, awareness activities, research, communication, events, education and other appropriate areas of Foundation work.

Why Volunteer?

Volunteering provides an opportunity to support meaningful community initiatives while sharing your knowledge and skills.

🤝

Serve Your Community

Use your time and abilities to contribute to initiatives that benefit communities.

📢

Raise Awareness

Help promote awareness about good governance, accountability and responsible citizenship.

💡

Share Your Skills

Bring your professional, creative, technical or organizational skills to appropriate activities.

Volunteer Opportunities

Depending on available activities and organizational needs, volunteers may support the following areas.

Community Outreach

Support constructive engagement and awareness activities within communities.

  • Community mobilization
  • Public awareness
  • Community dialogue
  • Citizen engagement

Research & Documentation

Support information gathering, documentation and development of useful community resources.

  • Research assistance
  • Report preparation
  • Data collection
  • Documentation

Media & Communication

Help communicate Foundation activities and educational messages through appropriate channels.

  • Content creation
  • Photography
  • Social media support
  • Graphic design

Events & Programmes

Assist with the planning and coordination of suitable Foundation events and programmes.

  • Event support
  • Registration assistance
  • Logistics support
  • Community coordination

How to Volunteer

Getting started is simple.

1

Contact Us

3

4

Get Involved

Participate responsibly in approved Foundation activities.
