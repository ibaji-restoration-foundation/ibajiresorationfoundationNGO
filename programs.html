OUR PROGRAMMES

Our programmes focus on good governance, advocacy, accountability, citizen participation, community development and sustainable initiatives.

What We Do

Turning our commitment to good governance and community development into practical programmes.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation develops and supports initiatives designed to encourage responsible leadership, informed citizenship, community participation and sustainable development.

Our programmes are intended to create opportunities for dialogue, awareness, advocacy and constructive engagement around issues affecting communities.

Our Governance Focus

Ibaji Restoration Foundation advocates for governance systems where citizens can participate, leaders are accountable and public affairs are conducted transparently and responsibly.

  • Transparency in public affairs
  • Accountability and responsible leadership
  • Citizen participation in governance
  • Respect for community needs and priorities
  • Constructive dialogue and peaceful advocacy
  • Sustainable and inclusive development
