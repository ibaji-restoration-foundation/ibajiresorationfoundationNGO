Our Programmes

Building stronger communities through good governance, accountability, citizen participation, advocacy and sustainable development initiatives.

Creating Positive Change in Ibaji

Ibaji Restoration Foundation is committed to promoting responsible leadership, accountability, transparency and sustainable community development.

Through our programmes, we work with citizens, community leaders, young people, women, civil society organisations and other stakeholders to identify challenges and promote practical solutions.

  • Promoting good governance
  • Strengthening citizen participation
  • Supporting community development
  • Promoting transparency and accountability
  • Advocating for sustainable development
What We Do

Our Core Programmes

Our programmes are designed to empower citizens, strengthen communities and promote responsible governance.

⚖

Good Governance

We promote responsible leadership, effective governance, public accountability and leadership that places the interests of citizens and communities first.

📢

Advocacy & Awareness

We create awareness around citizens' rights, public responsibilities, community issues and policies that affect the lives of people.

🏘

Community Development

We support initiatives that contribute to stronger, healthier and more sustainable communities across Ibaji and surrounding areas.

👥

Citizen Participation

We encourage citizens to participate actively in community decision-making and public affairs.

🔎

Accountability & Transparency

We advocate for transparency in public affairs and encourage institutions and leaders to remain accountable to the people.

🌱

Sustainable Development

We promote development approaches that address present needs while protecting opportunities and resources for future generations.

Advocacy for Good Governance

Good governance requires citizens who understand their rights and responsibilities and are willing to participate in the development of their communities.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation uses advocacy, public awareness, community engagement and constructive dialogue to encourage better leadership and stronger public institutions.

Our approach is peaceful, inclusive and focused on practical solutions that can improve the lives of citizens.

Future Focus

Areas We Intend to Support

As the Foundation grows, we intend to expand our activities into additional areas that directly impact communities.

Promoting environmental awareness and responsible community practices that protect natural resources.
