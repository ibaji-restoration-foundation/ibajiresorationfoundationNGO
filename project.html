Our Work

Projects & Community Initiatives

Our projects are designed to respond to the needs of communities while promoting responsible leadership, transparency and sustainable development.

Ibaji Restoration Foundation (NGO) is committed to supporting initiatives that contribute to the social, economic and institutional development of communities.

Through advocacy, community engagement, partnerships and development programmes, the Foundation seeks to empower citizens and encourage leaders to deliver meaningful and sustainable results.

Our project approach is community-focused, inclusive and guided by the principles of accountability, participation, responsible leadership and good governance.

Building Stronger Communities

Sustainable development begins with communities. We believe that citizens should have a voice in matters that affect their lives and should be active participants in the development process.

Our projects therefore encourage collaboration between citizens, community leaders, civil society organisations, government institutions and development partners.

  • Community participation and consultation
  • Responsible and accountable leadership
  • Advocacy for improved public services
  • Support for human capital development
  • Partnership for sustainable development
